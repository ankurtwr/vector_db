Here are the key notes from the pizza-making guide for a classic Margherita pizza:

### **Core Principles**
*   **Quality Ingredients:** Success relies on high-quality ingredients, specifically the mozzarella cheese.
*   **Italian Roots:** The Margherita was invented in 1889 to honor Queen Margherita of Italy. It features the colors of the Italian flag: Red (sauce), White (mozzarella), and Green (basil).

### **Preparation Steps**
*   **Surface Prep:** Grease your surface/tray with olive oil to prevent the dough from sticking to your hands and the base.
*   **Stretching:** Gently stretch the dough. It does not need to be a perfect circle; imperfections are part of its character.
*   **Docking:** Use a fork to prick holes (dock) in the dough before adding toppings. This prevents uneven rising and ensures an even cook.
*   **Sauce & Cheese:** Spread your homemade pizza sauce evenly, then top with shredded, high-quality mozzarella.

### **Baking & Finishing**
*   **Oven Temperature:** Bake in a preheated oven set to the **highest possible temperature**.
*   **The Basil Rule:** Add fresh basil **only after baking**. Adding it during the bake will cause the leaves to turn black.
*   **Final Touches:** Drizzle with a little olive oil. Optional additions include chili flakes and oregano to taste.

### **Pro-Tips**
*   **Dough Integrity:** Proper dough (that passes the "windowpane test") won't break, but handle it with care during stretching.
*   **Versatility:** If you don't have a specialized tray, a regular oven tray works just fine.
*   **No Oven?** The channel suggests there are alternative methods for those without an oven (covered in their next tutorial).
